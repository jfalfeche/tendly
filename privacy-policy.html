Tendly Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026 · Jubille Tricia Alfeche

Tendly is made by an independent developer, Jubille Tricia Alfeche ("we", "us"). It helps you keep track of your pets' care: health records, vet visits, medicines, walks, feeding, and a journal. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Using Tendly without an account

You can use Tendly in offline mode without signing in. Everything you enter then stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Information we collect when you sign in

When you create an account, what you enter is synced to our servers so you can reach it on other devices and keep it if you lose your phone.

InformationWhy we need it
Email address or phone number To create your account and sign you in. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address that provider shares with us.
Pet profiles and care records: names, species, weights, vaccines, preventive treatments, medicines, vet visits and clinics, feeding and breathing logs, reminders, and documents you upload To provide the app's features and sync them across your devices.
Photos: pet photos, journal photos, and your profile picture To show them in the app. We only get the photos you choose; Tendly never scans your photo library.
Precise location during walks To record the route and distance of a walk you start. Location is only used while you're tracking a walk, and the route is saved with that walk.
Journal entries and feedback you send us To store your journal, and to read and respond to your feedback.

We don't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Permissions on your device

  • Camera and photos: used only when you take or choose a photo, or save a walk map image to your library.
  • Location: used only to track walks you start.
  • Notifications: used for the reminders you set up. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

You can turn off any of these in iOS Settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Who processes your information

We use a small number of service providers to run Tendly. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage, and sign-in (hosted in Singapore).
  • Apple and Google: handle sign-in if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies.
  • An SMS delivery provider: receives your phone number only to deliver one-time sign-in codes if you sign in with a phone number.
  • OpenStreetMap: supplies the map tiles on walk maps. Their servers see your IP address and the map area being displayed.
  • Google Fonts: may supply the app's fonts, which exposes your IP address to Google.

Authorized Tendly support staff can access account data when that's needed to investigate a problem you've reported or to keep the service secure. We don't share your information with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. If you delete a record in the app, it's hidden everywhere the next time the app syncs, but we keep it marked as deleted so your other devices can catch up. All of these records are permanently erased when you delete your account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under You → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, every record synced to it, and every photo and document you uploaded. It also removes that data from the device you used to delete it. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within 30 days.

If you can't reach the app, email us at tendlypet@gmail.com and we'll delete your account for you.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Each account can only read and write its own records, and that rule is enforced on the server.

Children

Tendly is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. You can view and edit most of it directly in the app. For anything else, contact us at tendlypet@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app before they take effect.
